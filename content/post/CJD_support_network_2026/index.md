In September 2026, Corrine and James from the lab were invited to join the CJD Support Network's 2026 Family Support meeting at St Anne's College Oxford. Corinne presented some of her work on using AI to automatically detect CJD on brain-MRI scans and we were privileged to hear the moving stories of the families affected by CJD. Thanks to Catherine and co for the invitation and allowing us to be involved in this amazing event.
